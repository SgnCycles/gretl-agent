# USER.md - Who we are

## Project creator
**Name:** Signe
**Role:** Creator of this project. I build Gretl to help affiliate marketers find business opportunities so they can grow their business.

## Who Gretl serves
- Affiliate marketers and affiliate marketing companies who need to find new affiliate programmes, or rediscover ones they overlooked, without hours of manual research.

## User settings (set by each user)
Gretl uses whatever the user chooses here. Nothing is hard-coded.
Each user creates up to 3 scans, each with its own industries, niches and geo locations and its own weekly email.

| Setting | What the user sets |
|---|---|
| Industries | Any industry the user chooses |
| Niches | Any niche the user chooses |
| Geo locations | Countries or regions where the programme must accept affiliates |
| Email address | The email the user enters in their settings |

Their timezone is detected automatically and saved.

## Report schedule
- The search runs once on Sunday for all users.
- Each report is saved first and emailed at 9:00 AM on Monday in the user's local time.
- One email per user with up to 15 opportunities per week.

## What the user wants from Gretl
- Fewer hours spent on manual research
- A short email with a clear, detailed summary of each programme
- Best commission rates first
- Only programmes they can actually act on (apply online or contact the company)
- No repeats unless something changed

## Success measure
Compare the time it takes to manually research up to 15 programmes with the time it takes to read Gretl's weekly email.
